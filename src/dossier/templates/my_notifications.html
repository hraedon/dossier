my notifications

dossier nudges, it does not nag. When an event needs you — a review awaiting your accept, an item returned, an integrity failure — a notification is emitted to the configured sink (agent-wake), carrying a deep link to the exact page. Here you choose which classes reach you and whether they are immediate or batched into a digest. Review-actionable events deep-link to the work item; an integrity failure deep-links to the integrity report (the recovery surface).
